# Nijatva Contacts — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Nijatva Contacts is a private contacts app for Android.

## Data We Collect

**None.** The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. There are no analytics, no ads and no trackers.

## Data On Your Device

The app reads and writes contacts through Android's own contacts database, which stays on your device. Settings (sort order, preferences) are stored locally and are removed when you uninstall.

## Optional Zoho sync

If you choose to sign in with Zoho, the app requests only the `zohocontacts.contactapi.READ` scope to read your Zoho contacts and copy them into your device contacts. Tokens are stored locally on the device. Nothing is sent anywhere else. This feature is entirely optional — the app works fully offline without it.

## Permissions

- **Contacts (read/write)** — to display, import and export contacts
- **Internet** — used only for the optional Zoho sync

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on this repository.
